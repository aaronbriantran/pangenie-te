# 25 June 2026

I have tried multiple approaches in the past few days for getting the chunking to work in Snakemake for SHAPEIT. For various reasons, this is a bad idea.

<ol>
<li>SHAPEIT likes having lists go into files that get passed in for ligation in an ordered format.</li>
<li>SHAPEIT really wants to use GLIMPSE only as its method of chunking, and tracking that across Snakemake rules is tough</li>
<li>GLIMPSE requires AC/AN INFO tags in the VCF to work appropriately, but Pangenie genotypes and the HPRC VCF don't have them, and filling them in is hard to do since I don't have the bcftools plugin for that.</li>
</ol>

I decided to cut my losses. After all, I don't strictly need chunking, so I decide to do the easiest thing and directly split the reference VCF by chromosome and phase on phase_common an entire chromosome. Let's not make things more complicated than they should be!

I have largely finished implementing the Snakemake workflow, fixing some small nagging issues. One interesting issue is that for bcftools/1.19 and below (I am running bcftools/1.18), --threads and --write-index cannot be used together. I reimplemented a separate bcftools indexing step for all commands that use --write-index.
