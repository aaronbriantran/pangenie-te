## Using vg construct for the breakpoint analysis

Using autoindex produces graphs that align the assemblies in their own way, thus affecting the representation of structural variants (their placement in the genome). To coerce variants in the graph to look like their origin VCF, we must manually build indices and perform construction.

To do so, I modify the scripts/map_reads.sh to map reads to the manually created graph and created scripts/pack_and_gtype.sh, which does the same pack augmentation/read support calculation but does it on a genotyped graph. This yields results/pack_and_gtype/F1/F1.vcf, without use of the -a flag in the variant genotyping (i.e. no genotyping of homozygous reference loci).

Use of bcftools stats indicates very few insertions genotyped in general (only 30000 or so) and there are only 103 insertions greater than 60 bp.

A little exploring in our genotyped VCF shows no entries existing in other chromosomes except for chr22! Furthermore, there are clearly homozygous reference loci genotyped here, despite missing the -a flag.

My most immediate guess here is that the usage of -v flags in parallel simply does not work in genotyping. In the pack_and_gtype.sh script:

```bash
VCF_FLAGS=()
for CHROM in {1..22}; do
   VCF_FLAGS+=(-v results/create_sample_vcfs/${1}_chr${CHROM}.vcf.gz)
done
```

We continuously append new chromosomes, with the very last one being chr22. Then, these all get passed as flags, sequentially, to vg call. I think chr22 may simply be the last flag, thus overwriting every other choice rather than implicitly catting them for me or parallelizing.

Simply subsetting to chr22 and finding the stats:

```bash
bcftools view -r chr22 ../5_phasing/results/phased_complete.bcf | bcftools stats
```

Yields 103 insertion SVs (of all forms, not subsetting for alts but phased)

Evidently this suggests that genotyping supports basically all of the relevant indels in a phased panel. We have to validate two things: _that this works genome wide, across all chromosomes, and that this validation occurs via breakpoint aligned reads._
