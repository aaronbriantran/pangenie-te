- SHAPEIT can handle indels! Nonetheless, I should check to see what kind of variants I am or am not seeing.
- I shouldn't worry about using reads to fix phasing, because the chances that a variant's phasing can be fixed are pretty low. It requires a heterozygous site within read length, and variants generally happen at a rate of 1 per 1000 bp. Of course, at 302 bp pair ended, using reads is probably not going to work.
- I also shouldn't worry about the same site being phased differently *between* samples. What are the chances that two genomes share the same mutation at the same locus, but that their neighboring variants are so different that SHAPEIT thinks they exist on separate haplotype blocks? They probably don't.
- Juan also thinks that merging 1KG and HPRC panels together isn't going to do much. If it does, maybe I should try it?

Onto the Snakemake workflow:
